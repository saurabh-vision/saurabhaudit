When you copy text, ClipVault inspects the string locally on your Android device using regex patterns and lightweight parsers. It classifies clips into six distinct categories: plain text, web URLs, email addresses, phone numbers, color hex codes, and code snippets.

This on-device detection powers type-based filtering and quick actions, such as opening links in your browser or dialing a phone number with a single tap. All parsing runs entirely on your device with zero network calls.

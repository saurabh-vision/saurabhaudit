ClipVault includes an Android home screen widget that lets you view and paste recent clipboard items directly from your launcher. While convenient for daily snippets, Android widgets render directly on your unlocked home screen without prompting for biometric or passphrase authentication.

Anyone who picks up your unlocked phone can see items shown on the widget. For this reason, never pin passwords, private tokens, or recovery phrases to widgets. Keep sensitive credentials safely inside the encrypted vault tab.

Review the full security model in [How ClipVault encrypts your clips and vault](/posts/how-clipvault-encrypts-your-clips-and-vault/).
